Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

1. Who we are

Honec Company ("we", "our", "us") provides project planning, construction, renovation, and support services. This policy explains what personal data we collect when you use our website and how we use it.

2. Data we collect

We collect data you provide directly:

  • Account registration — name, email address, and password hash (stored securely by Supabase Auth).
  • OAuth sign-in — if you sign in with Google or GitHub, we receive your name, email, and profile picture from that provider.
  • Contact form — name, email, subject, and message.
  • Newsletter — email address only.
  • News comments and reactions — comment text and emoji reactions linked to your account.

We also collect limited technical data automatically:

  • IP address and browser user-agent (logged by Cloudflare for security and DDoS protection).
  • Pages visited and timestamps (standard Cloudflare analytics).

3. How we use your data

  • To create and manage your account.
  • To respond to contact form messages.
  • To send newsletter updates (only if you subscribed).
  • To display your name next to comments and reactions.
  • To detect abuse and enforce our Terms of Service (ban/suspend accounts).

4. Data storage and processors

Your data is stored in Supabase (PostgreSQL database hosted on AWS). Our website is served via Cloudflare Workers. We do not sell your data to third parties.

5. Data retention

Account data is retained until you request deletion. Contact messages are retained for up to 2 years. Newsletter subscriptions are retained until you unsubscribe.

6. Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time by emailing us at the address below. We will respond within 30 days.

7. Cookies

We set one functional cookie (__csrf) to protect against cross-site request forgery. No advertising or tracking cookies are used. Supabase stores a session token in localStorage to keep you signed in.

8. Security

All data is transmitted over HTTPS. Passwords are hashed by Supabase Auth and never stored in plain text. Row-Level Security (RLS) policies in our database ensure users can only access data they are authorised to see.

9. Contact

For privacy enquiries, please use the Contact page or email us directly. We do not have a dedicated DPO but will handle all requests promptly.
